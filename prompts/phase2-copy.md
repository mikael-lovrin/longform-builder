# Phase 2 — Long-form copy

Goal: write one copy per cell, at the angle's length, with the Xquad voices, and with the locks obeyed at the source instead of in the correction pass.

## Before writing

Read, in this order:
1. `knowledge/longform-anatomy.md` — the 16 movements
2. `knowledge/persuasive-prose.md` — **the 15 sentence techniques**. This is what separates a long form read to the end from one abandoned at the third block
3. `knowledge/production-locks.md` — the 12 locks
4. `knowledge/xquad-routing.md` — who writes what
5. The cell in `matrix.md`

The anatomy gives the order, the locks give the content, the prose gives the sound. All three layers are mandatory: a copy that nails the structure and misses the rhythm does not get read, and copy that is not read does not convert.

Also read at least **two real examples** from `support/` or from the round's examples folder before the first batch. Not to copy: to calibrate length and paragraph rhythm.

## Writing order

Write **by angle, not by level**: B1-A, B1-B, B1-C, then B2-A... That way the three columns of the same angle are born differentiated on purpose, with the same material fresh in your head.

## Draft format

One file per cell in `drafts/`, named `B{n}-{level}.md`:

```markdown
---
id: AA BRAND-SKU T101-B1-A

level: A
mechanism_position: late
voices: [andre-chaperon, blair-warren, cialdini]

chars: 0
---

## PRIMARY TEXT

(the long-form copy)

## FIRST 3 LINES (what shows before see more)

(copy the first 3 lines verbatim and count the characters)

## LINK HEADLINE

## LINK DESCRIPTION

## CTA

## CONSTRUCTION NOTES

- Movements used: (list)
- Where each proof modality appears
- Enemy block: where it went
```

## Writing rules that decide the result

**Short paragraphs.** One idea per block, frequent line breaks. On mobile a six-line block is a wall. The pieces that scale run one to three line blocks.

**The first three lines are a headline, not an introduction.** Write them last, once the copy exists, and test them: do they raise the question without handing over the answer? Do they fit in 250 to 350 characters?

**No em dashes and no quotation marks (LOCK 11).** A spoken line is marked with a line break. That changes how you write dialogue and it has to happen at the source, not in a find and replace.

**The narrator never declares his own result (LOCK 9).** If "I felt incredible" shows up, rewrite it as someone outside noticing.

**Every number is real (LOCK 10).** Lab value between 350 and 412, doses from the label, the current dollar price. Nothing invented.

**Enemy block mandatory:** the blue pill, TRT, the underdosed supplement, the doctor who says normal. All four, in every batch, but as enemies and never as the main villain (LOCK 5).

## Execution difference by level

- **A** — opens on the scene. The reader has to place himself in a room, at an hour, next to an object. The mechanism is the relief that arrives in the middle.
- **B** — opens on the graveyard. The reader has to see himself in the list of what he already tried. The mechanism retroactively explains why none of it could have worked. *This column may enter with prior negative evidence; write the best possible version of it anyway, because the point of the test is to see whether the format changes the behavior.*
- **C** — opens on the reveal. The mechanism is news in the first line. The pain enters afterwards, as confirmation of what the news said. **The order of the movements inverts.**

## Check before closing each batch

- [ ] It sits inside the angle's character band
- [ ] The 3 declared proof modalities actually appear in the text
- [ ] Product named (LOCK 2), late (LOCK 3), no trick vocabulary
- [ ] Dollar price present (LOCK 6)
- [ ] Third-party validation present (LOCK 9)
- [ ] Lab value with a number (LOCK 10)
- [ ] Zero em dashes, zero quotation marks (LOCK 11)
- [ ] Complete enemy block
- [ ] The CTA sends to the round's destination with a direct verb

## When the batches are done

Run the Xquad psychology layer (Tier 1E) over the set, not batch by batch: what matters is whether the 15 copies sound like 15 different people or like the same person wearing 15 hats. If they sound the same, the round will measure noise.
