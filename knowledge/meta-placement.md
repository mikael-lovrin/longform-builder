# Meta placement — the 5 fields of a long form static

> Visual reference: screenshots of a competitor's ads pulled from the public Ad Library, kept in the round's `support/` folder. Every batch delivers all five fields. Missing any one of them blocks the upload.

## How the ad shows up

```
[Page Name]  ·  Sponsored
{PRIMARY TEXT — first 3 lines visible}  ... See more
┌──────────────────────────┐
│                          │
│        1:1 IMAGE         │
│                          │
└──────────────────────────┘
DOMAIN.COM
{LINK HEADLINE}                           [ CTA BUTTON ]
{LINK DESCRIPTION}
```

---

## 1. Primary text (the long-form copy)

The whole piece, 2,500+ characters. See `longform-anatomy.md`.

**What decides the result is the first 3 lines**, because that is all that shows before see more. They have to:
- work as a closed block, with meaning of their own
- create the question without handing over the answer
- fit roughly **125 characters per line** on mobile, so plan for about **250 to 350 characters** before the cut

Patterns that work, from the collected examples:

| Pattern | Real example |
|---|---|
| Isolated shocking line | the wife's confession delivered in line one |
| Intimate timestamp | it is 1:47 in the morning, he is asleep, I cannot stop smiling |
| Serial normality question | is it normal to start going soft in the middle of sex after 35 |
| Reluctant concession | my wife nagged me to post this. I told her it was nobody's business |
| Inverted status statement | my wife became a different woman in bed when I got hard again |

**No em dashes and no quotation marks** (LOCK 11). A spoken line is marked with a line break.

## 2. Image

1:1, no embedded text, no product, no logo. See `casting-longform.md`.

## 3. Link headline

Shows in bold under the image. **40 to 70 characters.**

It does not repeat the copy's opening: it **offers the answer the copy promised**, or asks the question the quiz will answer.

Real collected examples:
- Is It Normal To Lose Your Erections After 35? A Urologist Answers.
- How This Simple Trick Helped Me Avoid Losing Real Size.
- Here's The Natural Solution That Made My Wife Obsessed Again
- Support stamina and blood flow every day

When the round's destination is a quiz, the preferred headline is a **diagnostic question**, because it matches what the reader finds on the other side.

**Watch LOCK 3:** "simple trick" is competitor vocabulary, not ours. `trick_hack` is the worst group in the batch (0.195). Do not copy that pattern.

## 4. Link description

The line under the headline, gray, smaller. **60 to 110 characters.** Frequently truncated, so the value has to sit at the front.

Job: numeric social proof, or an explanation of what happens on click.

Real examples:
- Over 110,000 men reported the same frustrations before they started taking...
- By evaluating your answers in this quiz, we'll be able to understand your situation...

For a quiz destination, the description explains the quiz. For a PDP, it carries a social proof number.

## 5. CTA button

When the round goes to a quiz, the button is **Learn More**, because that is what Meta offers for a quiz destination.

> Careful with how you read LOCK 8: it says a **direct-purchase CTA beats learn more (0.412 against 0.191)**. That number compares *different funnels*, not buttons. When the destination is a quiz by test design, the button is Learn More and **the CTA compression happens inside the copy**, which has to push to the quiz with a direct verb, never with a lukewarm invitation.
