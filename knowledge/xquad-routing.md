# Xquad routing — which voice writes what

> Squad: `Copy-Master-Xquads/` (32 specialists plus `copy-master-chief`). Gate: `checklists/output-quality.md` (COPY-M-CL-001). Catalog: `data/routing-catalog.yaml`.
>
> Long form static falls under the catalog's `sales_letter` domain (primary `gary-halbert`, secondary `john-carlton`), but a 5,000-character sales letter in a Facebook feed is not a direct-mail sales letter. The routing below is that domain specialized for this format.

## How to use it

Before writing each batch:
1. Pull the angle's **base voice** and its **support voice**.
2. Write the critical blocks with the specialist named in the block table.
3. Run the **psychology layer** (Tier 1E) over the closed draft.
4. Declare in the draft header which voices were used.

Do not mix more than three voices in one batch. Past that it becomes a patchwork and the gate fails it on tone consistency.

## Psychology layer (Tier 1E) — mandatory

Runs over the closed draft, before the quality gate:

- `robert-cialdini` — check which triggers are live and whether any is being forced
- `blair-warren` — check the absolution: the reader has to feel the fault is not his, without the copy calling him a victim
- `chris-voss` — check the unspoken objections, especially in female-voice copy, where the real objection is how do I give this to him without bruising his pride

## The 8-point gate — how it applies here

The squad's `output-quality.md` applies in full. Three CRITICAL items deserve a translation for this format:

| Gate item | Long form static translation |
|---|---|
| *Headline stops the reader* | The 3 lines before the cut. If they do not raise the question, it fails |
| *Every sentence compels the reader to read the next one* | Test by paragraph, not by sentence. A dense block on mobile kills the read |
| *Scarcity or urgency is logical and believable, not manufactured* | Matches LOCK 7. The legitimate urgency is the biological window, never stock running out |

**Known conflict:** the gate asks for *price anchoring* and *risk reversal*, and LOCK 6 requires a dollar price in the copy. Those two agree. But the gate also accepts "believable" scarcity, while LOCK 7 forbids any manufactured scarcity on this account. **Where they conflict, the house lock wins**, because it came from the operation's own numbers.
